Flag League

A daily physics-simulation league between the world's flags.

Every day, national flags race in a physics arena — rotating rings, shrinking rings, blades, gravity wells, portals, plinko boards, race tracks, health-bar battles and knockout brackets. Points from every race build a season table; four-week seasons end with a final, and the year ends with a World Championship.

Every race is a real physics simulation. Results are not scripted: the random seed is chosen only for pacing, before any country is assigned to a starting position, and each video lists its seed and engine version.

About this app

Flag League Uploader is the channel owner's own tool. It renders the videos on the owner's server and publishes them to the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It has no other users and no sign-up.
